# Pokegochi v2.2

Pokegochi v2.2 expands the Generation III virtual-pet experience while keeping
the permanent save, battle rules, and visual language of the existing V2 game.

## World and real-time features

- Phone-clock synchronization through the Android Bluetooth updater.
- Time-of-day lighting and species-only ambient behavior such as sleeping.
- Occasional rain, strong wind, hail, and Sunny Day across the complete Home
  scene. Local battles inherit the active weather and announce it on entry;
  multiplayer battles remain neutral.
- A FireRed-style Berry Garden with authentic berries, planting, watering,
  growth, harvesting, and persistent plots.
- Multiple held berries of the same type can be carried and consumed across a
  battle while stock remains.
- At 10% battery or less the device saves and enters dormant protection, only
  resuming after the charge rises above the threshold.

## Pokémon, items, and battles

- Mega form selection for species with more than one Mega Evolution, replacing
  the previous sex-based X/Y choice.
- Additional Generation I-III Mega forms without Dynamax, Terastallization,
  Z-Moves, or other later battle systems.
- Species-specific held items: Light Ball, Lucky Punch, Thick Club, Stick,
  Metal Powder, Quick Powder, DeepSeaTooth, DeepSeaScale, and Soul Dew.
- Lucky Egg is now a permanent party-wide experience bonus after it is earned
  and no longer occupies a held-item slot.
- Enemy and wild Pokémon consume PP and use Struggle after exhausting every
  usable move.
- Battle Tower completions have an independent 0.5% Master Ball reward chance.

## Interface and activities

- Settings reorganized into tabs, with phone-synchronized clock status and
  separate Finger and Stylus touch profiles.
- Improved touch filtering and corrected the displaced first touch after
  returning to Home.
- Box sorting by total EVs, with level as the tie-breaker.
- BOX → PARTY cards keep a large level label and a green/orange/red HP bar
  without cramped numeric HP text.
- Clearer EV editor states for applied and pending values.
- Bag and Shop pagination wraps in both directions; exhausted Shop stock no
  longer resets when the screen is reopened.
- Cards scores a completed round by the number of cards clicked. Berry Catch
  still records the time required to collect ten authentic berries.
- Kids Games received corrected iconography and game-derived visuals.

## Update and save compatibility

- Android updater version 2.2 supports clock synchronization and the current
  signed firmware/asset update protocol.
- Save data remains persistent and migrates through the existing redundant
  save system. The wireless protocol remains compatible with V2 devices.
