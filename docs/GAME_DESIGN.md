# Pokegochi game specification

This document is the current source of truth. The in-game language is English.

## Collection and permanent save

- A device has exactly one game. Production firmware never exposes New Game or Reset.
- Development-only factory reset remains compile-time gated.
- The Box stores up to 151 Pokemon and the active Pokegochi team has up to three.
- The player chooses Bulbasaur, Charmander, or Squirtle on the first start.
- Important actions are committed immediately to redundant persistent records.

## Battle categories

The engine distinguishes `Wild`, `Trainer`, `Gym`, and `PvP`. Capture is accepted
only when the engine state is `Wild`; hiding the Ball button is not the rule.

### Random wild encounter

- Generated by a persistent random clock; initial tuning is 1–3 hours.
- Contains wild Pokemon only and never grants additional loot.
- The player chooses any healthy selected-team member before it starts.
- The Pokemon can be captured and grants experience when defeated.
- Difficulty uses the highest level in the currently selected team.

### Player-initiated trainer battle

- Consumes one of three charges; one charge returns every three hours.
- The player chooses any healthy selected-team member before it starts.
- The trainer owns one, two, or three Pokemon and none can be captured.
- Difficulty uses the highest level in the currently selected team.
- It grants experience but no capture farming.
- A generated trainer retains a FireRed trainer identity/class. Its original front
  sprite, encounter presentation, and original encounter line are shown before combat.

## Gyms

- Gyms use fixed FireRed-inspired levels and never scale with the player.
- Every Gym battle has exactly three opponents. Leaders with larger original teams
  keep their three highest-level Pokemon; Brock and Misty are completed with the
  strongest suitable challenger Pokemon from their own FireRed Gym.
- They unlock and appear in canonical order.
- Losing leaves the same gym available for another attempt.
- Winning permanently records its Badge and enables the next gym.
- A completed gym never appears again.

Gym content and event timing remain separate from the generic battle engine.

## Bluetooth multiplayer

One shared connection layer supports PvP with selected teams and Pokemon trades.
A trade requires selection, proposal, validation, and confirmation by both devices.
It must be atomic: neither save removes a Pokemon until both peers confirm the same
transaction identifier and payload. Interrupted or mismatched transactions abort.

## Care and recovery

- Feed and Play support regular interaction.
- Bath clears persistent combat status; cleanliness is not a timed need.
- A defeated Pokemon keeps progress and recovers automatically after about 2–3 hours.

## Deferred visual items

- Current Box and Pokedex icon candidates are rejected and must not ship. If no useful
  original icon exists, a user-supplied reference will be converted to matching pixel art.
- The complete Pokedex screen should reproduce FireRed's Kanto Pokedex as closely as
  possible: original window tiles, list layout, numbering, seen/caught markers, cursor,
  colors, Pokemon preview, and detail-page composition adapted to 320x240 touch input.
- If FireRed has no recognizable standalone Pokedex button graphic, derive a compact
  pixel-art device icon from `.downloads/pokedex-icon-reference.jpg`: red/orange folding
  body, circular lens/emblem, and screen silhouette. Do not use the previous mini-page icon.
- Home needs the actual rounded FireRed window tiles and a matching outer border.
- Battle grass composition and the attack command icon still require approval.
- The VS Seeker belongs to player-initiated encounters, not the attack command.
- Trainer and Gym Leader introductions use their original FireRed front sprites and
  encounter dialogue. Trainer identity, sprite, dialogue, and team template are one
  catalog entry and must not be randomized independently.
- Moves use their original battle-animation tile sequences, palettes, timing, and
  screen offsets whenever FireRed provides them.
- Throwing, opening, shaking, breaking free, and successful capture use the original
  Poke Ball animation family, with the correct ball graphic for each ball type.
- A missing or unsupported animation must be reported during asset-pack generation;
  silent replacement with a generic effect is not acceptable for the final build.
